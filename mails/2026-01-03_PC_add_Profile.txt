Dear PC Member,

Happy new year + thank you again for agreeing to serve as PC member for the SIGIR 2026 Full Papers Track.

We noticed that you haven't yet created your OpenReview profile, please do so as soon as possible.

And don't forget to add your DBLP link to your profile, which can be done as follows:

1. Log in to your OpenReview account and go to your profile page (click on your name in the top right corner and select "Profile")
2. Click on "Edit Profile" (towards the top)
3. Click on "Personal Links"
4. In the "DBLP URL" field, enter your DBLP link (of the form https://dblp.org/pid/... without .html in the end)
5. Click on "Add DBLP Papers to Profile" (right next to the "DBLP URL" field)

Let us know if you encounter any issues.
