Thank you very much for having submitted all your reviews by now. Thanks to reviewers like you, we can now start the discussion phase.

Each discussion will be led by a Senior Program Committee (SPC) member, called "Area Chair" in OpenReview. You will not see their name, unless they choose to reveal it or they send you a private email.

**Your most important task in the discussion phase is to be responsive.** So please watch out for emails from OpenReview, and please reply to them as soon as possible. SPCs have been instructed to nag you via email if you do not reply to their questions within a reasonable time frame. But please do not let it come to that.

**With the hard work of writing the reviews already behind you, the discussion phase can actually be fun and rewarding.** We hope that you experience a little bit of that.

For your information, we asked the SPCs to watch out for shallow or generic reviews, strong biases, unfounded claims, important aspects that are missed, and undue AI assistance. In particular, we sent them this checklist of [lazy reasons to reject a paper](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1I4eTHDaMQ2-khs5MEv_1BjEcvhsPWAIcZvTFzE124II/view#heading=h.f5czy5vvku9i).

When there is a disagreement with another reviewer, you should get to the point where you understand each other's arguments. This can result in agreement, but it can also result in a better understanding of the disagreement.

Please help us make the discussion phase a success by being responsive and by improving the reviews as much as possible. The better the reviews, the better the decisions, and the better the conference. Thank you!
