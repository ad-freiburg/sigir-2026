Dear {{firstname_or_fullname}},

We have now deployed the forms for rating the reviews and for submitting your
meta-reviews.

**Rating the reviews**: At the bottom of each review, to the right, you should
now see a button labeled "Rating". You can rate each review on a scale from 1
to 5, and there is also a text box for any optional comments you may have. The
rating will **not** be visible to the reviewers or to the authors. They will
eventually be made visible to the Senior Area Chairs assigned to the paper.

**Submitting meta-reviews**: At the top of the page for each paper, below the
panel with the title, abstract, etc., you should now see a button labeled "Meta
Review" on the right. Please carefully read the instructions. In particular, a
meta-review should **not** just summarize or average the reviews, but it should
separate the wheat from the chaff. Like the reviews, the meta-reviews will
eventually be made visible to the authors.

Both ratings and meta-reviews can be edited as often as you like until the
deadline, which is March 10 AoE.

Thank you very much for this. Your work as an SPC / Area Chair is absolutely
crucial for the quality and fairness of the review process. In particular, we
rely on you to identify and rectify any issues with the reviews.

As always, please do not hesitate to reach out to us if you have any questions.
