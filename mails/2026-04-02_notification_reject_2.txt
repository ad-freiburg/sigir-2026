### Accessing the Reviews

This year we received 1271 valid submissions (after withdrawals and desk rejections) and accepted 234 (18.4%) of them. Your paper was reviewed by at least three reviewers, who based on their previous publications are knowledgeable about the topic of your submission. The review process was led by a Senior Program Committee (SPC, called Area Chair in OpenReview) member, who was chosen for their relevant domain knowledge, led the discussion of your submission, and wrote the meta-review. A group of 46 Area Chairs (called Senior Area Chair in OpenReview) supervised the review process, discussed each submission with the SPC, and met with the Program Chairs to discuss all submissions that were controversial in any way.  The Program Chairs made the final decisions. 

You can find the final reviews for your paper on the submission page in OpenReview at {{submission}}.

### The Revise and Resubmit Process

Note that both ICTIR (Melbourne, July 25, see https://sigir-ictir.github.io/ictir2026/, submission deadline 16 April) and SIGIR-AP (Singapore, anticipated as being held in mid-December) allow “Revise and Resubmit” (R&R) submission of SIGIR full and short research papers. Under this track you are expected to have revised your paper in line with the SIGIR referee comments, and provided a brief discussion appendix as part of the re-submission that summarizes the changes that have been made and any other responses you might have to the SIGIR referees. Where possible, your paper will then be re-reviewed by one or more of the SIGIR referees who read it originally. Papers from other SIGIR Tracks can also be resubmitted to ICTIR or SIGIR-AP if they fall within their respective scopes, but as “new” submissions that will be fully re-reviewed.
